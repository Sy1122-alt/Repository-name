## 专题一 · 词汇与语法结构（单选）

### A. 2023 四川专升本真题（精选）

**真题 1.** I'm leaving in \_\_\_\_ minute. In \_\_\_\_ fact, I'll leave when I turn off the lights.
- A. /; the　B. a; /　C. a; a　D. the; the
- **答案：B**　解析：in a minute（一会儿）；in fact（事实上）固定搭配，不加冠词。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 2.** Please turn to Page \_\_\_\_, and look at the \_\_\_\_ picture.
- A. Two; second　B. Two; two　C. second; two　D. second; second
- **答案：A**　解析：Page Two（编号用基数词）、the second picture（第几张用序数词）。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 3.** The kids learned that light \_\_\_\_ faster than sound.
- A. travel　B. traveled　C. travels　D. has traveled
- **答案：C**　解析：客观真理用一般现在时，主语 light 第三人称单数。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 4.** The photo exhibition was held to \_\_\_\_ people's environmental awareness.
- A. rise　B. give　C. arise　D. raise
- **答案：D**　解析：raise awareness 提高意识；rise/arise 是不及物动词。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 5.** The famous lawyer made a speech \_\_\_\_ the education law.
- A. in　B. on　C. at　D. for
- **答案：B**　解析：a speech on... 关于……的演讲。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 6.** — We need a drink. — \_\_\_\_! Let's have one.
- A. Greatly　B. Simply　C. Possibly　D. Exactly
- **答案：D**　解析：Exactly 表示"正是如此/太对了"。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 7.** Neither you nor I am the expert on this issue, \_\_\_\_?
- A. are we　B. aren't we　C. do we　D. don't we
- **答案：A**　解析：反意疑问句：陈述含否定词 neither...nor，附加问句用肯定；be 动词与主语 we 一致用 are。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 8.** The hall was named after the professor \_\_\_\_ had made a great contribution to the university.
- A. which　B. who　C. whose　D. whom
- **答案：B**　解析：先行词 professor，从句缺主语 → who。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 9.** Hardly had I put down my cellphone \_\_\_\_ it rang again.
- A. as　B. than　C. after　D. when
- **答案：D**　解析：Hardly...when... 一……就……（固定搭配，Hardly 开头部分倒装）。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 10.** Peter won first prize in the Chinese Speech Competition. He \_\_\_\_ have studied very hard.
- A. may　B. should　C. must　D. could
- **答案：C**　解析：must have done 表示对过去的肯定推测"一定"。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 11.** The AI engineer, as well as his team members, \_\_\_\_ to attend the seminar on ChatGPT last month.
- A. was invited　B. were invited　C. have been invited　D. has been invited
- **答案：A**　解析：as well as 不影响主谓一致，主语 The AI engineer 单数；last month 用过去时被动。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 12.** The teacher explained it again \_\_\_\_ he could make himself better understood.
- A. now that　B. even if　C. so that　D. as far as
- **答案：C**　解析：so that 表目的"以便"。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 13.** The \_\_\_\_ of blood always makes her frightened.
- A. sight　B. view　C. look　D. glance
- **答案：A**　解析：the sight of blood 看见血。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 14.** As we arrived too early, \_\_\_\_ of the rooms were ready.
- A. all　B. none　C. any　D. both
- **答案：B**　解析：来得太早，所以"没有一间"准备好 → none。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 15.** When he was a child, the scientist was \_\_\_\_ about how life on earth began.
- A. curious　B. anxious　C. careful　D. hopeful
- **答案：A**　解析：be curious about 对……好奇。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 16.** — Have you completed your report yet? — No, but I \_\_\_\_ on it all day yesterday.
- A. would work　B. had worked　C. have worked　D. was working
- **答案：D**　解析：yesterday + all day 表示过去某时段持续 → 过去进行时。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 17.** \_\_\_\_ I know, they will go to Chengdu to watch the World University Games this summer.
- A. As though　B. Even if　C. As soon as　D. As far as
- **答案：D**　解析：As far as I know 据我所知。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 18.** Never in my life \_\_\_\_ such a wonderful lion dance.
- A. have I seen　B. I have seen　C. did I see　D. I saw
- **答案：A**　解析：Never 开头的否定倒装 + 现在完成时。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 19.** The school \_\_\_\_ great importance to students' mental health.
- A. attaches　B. takes　C. pays　D. offers
- **答案：A**　解析：attach importance to 重视（固定搭配）。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 20.** They gave a(n) \_\_\_\_ broadcast while the talent show was going on.
- A. living　B. live　C. alive　D. lively
- **答案：B**　解析：live broadcast 现场直播（live 作形容词读 /laɪv/）。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 21.** \_\_\_\_ it was only 5 p.m., the street lights in the northeast of the city were already on.
- A. Although　B. However　C. Since　D. For
- **答案：A**　解析：虽然才下午 5 点，灯却亮了 → 让步状语从句 Although。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 22.** Lily has made an \_\_\_\_ to meet her science teacher next Monday.
- A. attention　B. approval　C. agreement　D. appointment
- **答案：D**　解析：make an appointment 预约。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 23.** It is high time you \_\_\_\_ smoking.
- A. give up　B. gave up　C. must give up　D. will give up
- **答案：B**　解析：It is high time + 过去式（虚拟语气）。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 24.** Weather \_\_\_\_, the foreign guests will visit the Panda Base.
- A. permits　B. permitted　C. permitting　D. is permitted
- **答案：C**　解析：独立主格结构 Weather permitting（天气允许的话）。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 25.** The manager had his assistant \_\_\_\_ a presentation for the meeting with the clients.
- A. preparing　B. to prepare　C. prepared　D. prepare
- **答案：D**　解析：have sb. do sth. 让某人做某事。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 26.** \_\_\_\_ finds the lost car will get a gift as a reward.
- A. Whomever　B. Whoever　C. No matter whom　D. No matter who
- **答案：B**　解析：Whoever 引导主语从句（作从句主语）；No matter who 只能引导让步状语从句。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 27.** We were excited to learn Prof. Tu Youyou became the \_\_\_\_ scientist that won the Nobel Prize.
- A. Chinese woman first　B. first Chinese woman　C. Chinese first woman　D. first woman Chinese
- **答案：B**　解析：形容词排序，first 在国籍前：the first Chinese woman scientist。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 28.** I'd like to take \_\_\_\_ of this opportunity to thank you for your support.
- A. care　B. advantage　C. note　D. hold
- **答案：B**　解析：take advantage of 利用。
- 来源：2023 真题

**真题 29.** Traveling is becoming \_\_\_\_ popular in China as more and more people have their own cars.
- A. importantly　B. successfully　C. increasingly　D. mostly
- **答案：C**　解析：increasingly popular 越来越受欢迎。
- 来源：2023 真题

## 专题三 · 写作模板（观点/议论文，≥100 词）

**万能结构模板**

```
[开头] Nowadays, there is a growing concern about the issue of ________.
Different people hold different opinions. (引出话题)

[中间] On the one hand, some people argue that ________. (观点一+理由)
On the other hand, others believe that ________. (观点二+理由)
Besides, ________. (补充论证)

[结尾] In my opinion, ________. Only in this way can we ________. (我的观点+号召)
```

**高频句型（开头）**
- As far as I'm concerned, ...（就我而言）
- It goes without saying that ...（不言而喻）
- Recently, the problem of ... has aroused people's concern.
- There are different opinions among people as to ...

**高频句型（中间/结尾）**
- First of all, ... In addition, ... Last but not least, ...
- It is widely believed that ...
- In my opinion, we should value it and make full use of it.
- Only in this way can we improve ourselves.

**常考话题参考**：online learning（在线学习）、environmental protection（环保）、part-time jobs（兼职）、college life（大学生活）、sports（运动）、healthy lifestyle（健康生活）

## 专题四 · 2022 四川专升本真题（词汇语法 + 翻译 + 写作）

**真题 40.** It is reported that ___ president will have dinner with ___ President O'Connor tomorrow.
- A. the; the　B. a; a　C. the; /　D. /; /
- **答案：C**　解析：第一个 the 特指"这位总统"；President+人名 作头衔称呼时不加冠词。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 41.** It's only ___ from my school, so I can go on foot.
- A. ten minute's walk　B. ten minutes' walk　C. ten minutes walk　D. ten minute walk
- **答案：B**　解析：复数名词所有格在 s 后加撇号：ten minutes' walk。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 42.** Put on your coat, ___ you will catch a cold.
- A. and　B. but　C. or　D. so
- **答案：C**　解析：祈使句 + or + 结果："否则"。and 表顺承、but 表转折、so 表因果。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 43.** It is said that the author ___ by the end of this year.
- A. will finish　B. will be finish　C. will have finished　D. will have been finished
- **答案：C**　解析：by + 将来时间 → 将来完成时 will have done。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 44.** If you had done your homework yesterday, you ___ how to answer the question now.
- A. know　B. knew　C. will know　D. would know
- **答案：D**　解析：混合虚拟语气——条件句表过去（had done），主句表现在（would do）。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 45.** They must have met with their guests last night, ___?
- A. haven't they　B. didn't they　C. mustn't they　D. couldn't they
- **答案：B**　解析：must have done 表对过去推测，反意问句用 didn't（或 haven't 依据语境，本句有时间状语 last night 用 didn't）。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 46.** No sooner had he sat down ___ somebody knocked on the door.
- A. when　B. than　C. then　D. it
- **答案：B**　解析：No sooner...than... 固定搭配，"一……就……"。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 47.** The manager together with some workers ___ visiting the new company now.
- A. are　B. is　C. have been　D. has been
- **答案：B**　解析：就远原则——主语是 The manager（单数），together with 不影响谓语。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 48.** Some mountain areas, ___ the terrible road condition, are hard to arrive.
- A. but for　B. in spite of　C. with regard to　D. due to
- **答案：D**　解析：due to 由于（表原因）；in spite of 尽管；but for 要不是；with regard to 关于。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 49.** Poor ___ she was, she never accepted anything for nothing.
- A. although　B. though　C. so　D. yet
- **答案：B**　解析：as/though 引导让步状语从句可倒装：Poor though she was = Though she was poor。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 50.** 翻译：Please believe that the last thing I want to do is to be late and offend you and the guests from the head office.
- **答案：请你相信，我最不愿意做的事情就是迟到并冒犯您和总部来的客人。**　解析：the last thing 意为"最不愿意做的事"；head office 总部。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 51.** 翻译：He realized that leaving the bird there was equal to signing its death sentence, because it might be eaten by predators.
- **答案：他意识到，把鸟留在那里无异于让它等死，因为它可能会被食肉动物吃掉。**　解析：be equal to 等于/无异于；death sentence 死亡判决；predator 食肉动物。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 52.** 翻译：Young people's attitudes towards side jobs are not all the same.
- **答案：年轻人对副业的态度不尽相同。**　解析：attitude towards 对……的态度；side job 副业。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 53.** 翻译：In space, the astronauts carried out experiments to demonstrate interesting physical phenomena, such as "disappearing buoyancy" and "water ball".
- **答案：在太空中，宇航员通过实验展示了有趣的物理现象，如"浮力消失"和"水球"。**　解析：carry out experiments 做实验；buoyancy 浮力。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题

**真题 54.** 写作：假设你是李夏，是第 31 届世界大学生运动会的志愿者，组委会安排你给来自世界各地的运动员写一封英文信，推荐四川的一个知名景点。内容包括：①景点介绍 ②推荐理由 ③注意事项。参考词汇：volunteer, recommend, scenic spot。
- **答案（参考范文）：** Dear friends, Welcome to Chengdu! I am a volunteer for the 31st World University Games. The organizing committee has asked me to recommend a famous scenic spot to you. One I strongly recommend is the Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding, since Chengdu is the home to pandas. There you can closely observe pandas and even watch female pandas nursing their cubs, which can further your understanding of how to protect these beautiful but endangered creatures. Besides, you'd better go there early in the morning, because it is very hot at noon and pandas will go to sleep inside the house then. May you have a good day!
- 解析：范文按应用文书信结构组织，包含称呼、景点介绍、推荐理由、注意事项和结尾祝愿，覆盖题目要求的三个内容点。
- 来源：2022 四川专升本英语真题（参考例文）

## 专题五 · 完形填空专项（含 Passage 材料）

**Passage（完形篇一 · My Father）**：My father often works very hard. And he has little time to see a film. Here I'll tell you a funny story about him. One afternoon, when he finished his work and was about to go home, he found a film ticket under the glass on his desk. He thought he happened to have not much work to do that day and it was quite wonderful to pass the evening at the cinema. So he came back home and quickly finished his supper. Then he said good-bye to us and left. But to our surprise, he came back about half an hour later. I asked him what was the matter. He smiled and told us about a funny thing that had happened at the cinema. When my father was sitting in his seat, a woman came to my father's and said that the seat was hers. My father was surprised. He took out the ticket and looked at it carefully. It was Row 17, Seat 3. And then he looked at the seat. It was the same. So he asked her to show her ticket. She took out the ticket at once and the seat shown in it was Row 17, Seat 3. Why? What's the matter with all this? While they were wondering, suddenly the woman said, "The colors of the tickets are different." So they looked at the tickets more carefully. After a while, my father said, "Oh, I'm sorry, I made a mistake. My ticket is for the film a month ago. Take this seat, please." With these words, he left the cinema.

**练习 55.** 完形：My father often works very hard. And he has ___ to see a film.
- A. little money　B. much money　C. little time　D. much time
- **答案：C**　解析：由下文可知父亲忙得没时间看电影。
- 来源：完形填空专项（篇一）

**练习 56.** 完形：Here I'll tell you ___ about him.

## 专题七 · 语法单选 + 词汇辨析专项

**练习 113（语法·时态）** She ______ in this company since 2010.
- A. worked　B. has worked　C. works　D. had worked
- **答案：B**　解析：since + 过去时间点，主句用现在完成时 has worked。
- 来源：自编自测（现在完成时）

**练习 114（语法·时态）** By the time he arrived, the meeting ______.
- A. began　B. has begun　C. had begun　D. begins
- **答案：C**　解析："by the time + 过去时"表示到过去某一时刻之前已完成，用过去完成时 had begun。
- 来源：自编自测（过去完成时）

**练习 115（语法·虚拟）** If I ______ you, I would accept the offer.
- A. am　B. was　C. were　D. be
- **答案：C**　解析：与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句，be 动词一律用 were。
- 来源：自编自测（虚拟语气）

**练习 116（语法·虚拟）** I wish I ______ more time yesterday.
- A. have　B. had　C. had had　D. have had
- **答案：C**　解析：wish 后用虚拟，对过去（yesterday）的愿望用过去完成时 had had。
- 来源：自编自测（虚拟语气）

**练习 117（语法·非谓语）** ______ from the top of the mountain, the city looks beautiful.
- A. See　B. Seeing　C. Seen　D. To see
- **答案：C**　解析：the city 与 see 是被动关系，过去分词 Seen 作状语表被动。
- 来源：自编自测（非谓语）

**练习 118（语法·非谓语）** He had his car ______ yesterday.
- A. repair　B. repaired　C. repairing　D. to repair
- **答案：B**　解析：have sth. done 表示"让别人做某事/遭受"，car 与 repair 被动，用过去分词 repaired。
- 来源：自编自测（非谓语）

**练习 119（语法·定语从句）** The book ______ cover is red is mine.
- A. which　B. whose　C. who　D. that
- **答案：B**　解析：cover 与 book 是所属关系，定语从句用 whose 作定语。
- 来源：自编自测（定语从句）

**练习 120（语法·定语从句）** This is the factory ______ we visited last year.
- A. where　B. which　C. who　D. whose
- **答案：B**　解析：visited 是及物动词，缺宾语，先行词是物用 which/that；where 是关系副词不能作宾语。
- 来源：自编自测（定语从句）

**练习 121（语法·倒装）** Never ______ I seen such a beautiful scene.
- A. have　B. had　C. do　D. did
- **答案：A**　解析：Never 等否定词位于句首引起部分倒装，seen 提示用现在完成时，助动词 have 提前。
- 来源：自编自测（倒装）

**练习 122（语法·倒装）** Only by working hard ______ you succeed.
- A. you will　B. will you　C. you can　D. can't you
- **答案：B**　解析："Only + 状语"位于句首，主句部分倒装，will you succeed。
- 来源：自编自测（倒装）

**练习 123（语法·主谓一致）** The number of students in our school ______ increasing.
- A. are　B. is　C. were　D. have been
- **答案：B**　解析：the number of + 复数名词作主语，谓语用单数 is。
- 来源：自编自测（主谓一致）

**练习 124（语法·主谓一致）** A number of students ______ playing on the playground.
- A. is　B. was　C. are　D. has been
- **答案：C**　解析：a number of + 复数名词意为"许多"，谓语用复数 are。
- 来源：自编自测（主谓一致）

**练习 125（词汇·辨析）** Hurry up! There is ______ time left.
- A. few　B. little　C. a few　D. a little
- **答案：B**　解析：time 不可数，用 little（几乎没有）符合催促语境；a little 表示"有一点"。
- 来源：自编自测（few/little 辨析）

**练习 126（词汇·辨析）** The sun ______ in the east every morning.
- A. raises　B. rises　C. arises　D. arouses
- **答案：B**　解析：rise 不及物"升起"（太阳升起）；raise 及物"举起/提高"；arise"出现"；arouse"唤起"。
- 来源：自编自测（rise/raise 辨析）

**练习 127（词汇·辨析）** Everyone went to the party ______ Tom, who was ill.
- A. besides　B. except　C. except for　D. apart from
- **答案：B**　解析：except"除……之外（不包括）"，Tom 生病没去，故用 except；besides 表示"除……之外还有"。
- 来源：自编自测（except/besides 辨析）

**练习 128（词汇·辨析）** I will call you ______ next week to discuss the plan.
- A. sometime　B. some time　C. sometimes　D. some times
- **答案：A**　解析：sometime"某时"（副词）；some time"一段时间"；sometimes"有时"；some times"几次"。
- 来源：自编自测（sometime 辨析）
